About

I am a Software Engineer and AI/ML Researcher holding an MSc in Artificial Intelligence from European University of Cyprus and a BSc in Applied Informatics from the University of Macedonia. I thrive at the convergence of resilient server-side software engineering, high-throughput database systems, and state-of-the-art machine learning models.

Currently, I provide technical and engineering support for the EU-funded research project SECASSURED (Horizon Europe) at Public Power Corporation S.A., designing end-to-end anomaly detection and diagnostics pipelines for electric vehicle (EV) charging stations.

Previously, at Ithaca Lab (University of Western Macedonia), I worked across European initiatives (AI4CYBER, TORNADO) and national programs (ASKOS), researching adversarial AI, Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS), honeypot infrastructures, and human-robot interaction.

My goal is to continue engineering scalable, automated, and mission-critical back-end platforms powered by Python, Docker, Kubernetes, and Modern MLOps.
